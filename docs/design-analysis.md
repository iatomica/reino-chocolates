# Design Analysis: El Reino de los Chocolates

## Brand Personality
- **Artisanal**: Handcrafted European chocolate traditions.
- **Nostalgic**: Vintage tin boxes, carriage engravings, wax seals.
- **Regal**: Antique gold crowns, refined typography, royal heraldry.
- **Warm**: Creamy backgrounds, melted chocolate mid-tones, rich caramel accents.
- **Argentine Heritage**: Crafted locally with dulce de leche and native cacao.

## Visual Hierarchy
1. **Primary Focal Point**: Vintage teal chocolate tin with gold carriage engraving.
2. **Secondary Focal Points**: Editorial headings ("Pequeños placeres, grandes historias"), caramel-dripping chocolate closeups.
3. **Background & Surrounding**: Muted teal blocks (`#3F706F`), warm cream surfaces (`#FAF5EB`), subtle horse line-art.
4. **Ornamentation**: Crown logotype, Victorian corner frames, flourish dividers (`☙ NUESTRA ESENCIA ❧`).
